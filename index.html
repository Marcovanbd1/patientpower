We are asking what patient communication looks like when it is treated as seriously as the clinical care itself.

The treatment plan gets scrutiny.
What the patient hears rarely does.

A course of antibiotics goes through dosing checks, safety netting and review. The words a patient takes home about it are usually whatever was left over at the end of a ten minute appointment.

The gap

Two things get written. Only one gets held to a standard

The clinical record

  • Structured fields, checked against guidelines
  • Reviewed and signed off by a clinician
  • Safety netting written into the plan
  • Held to a professional and legal standard
versus

What the patient takes home

  • Whatever was said out loud, if remembered
  • A leaflet, sometimes the right one
  • No one checking it was understood
  • Held to no standard at all

How Afternote closes it

Read by four people, not one

Every Afternote is shaped by four professional perspectives, the same rigour a complex case gets in an MDT, applied to the words a patient is sent home with.

Nurse

Is it safe and usable

Are the instructions something someone could actually follow at home, and does the safety netting say what to watch for.

Social worker

Can they act on it

Does the note account for who is around to help, what support exists, and what happens if there is no one.

Clinical psychologist

What is it like to read this

Is the tone right for someone who may be frightened, tired or overwhelmed, and is there anything left unsaid that needed saying.

Applied philosopher

Whose story is this

Does the note give the patient back some authorship of what happened to them, rather than leaving them as a subject of someone else's record.

A clinical note answers one question. This asks four.

What it actually produces

Same consultation, written for a different reader

Clinical record, for the record
Plan
Antibiotics twice daily for seven days. Oral steroids once daily for five days, with food. Continue existing medications.
Safety netting
If not better by Sunday, book Thursday or Friday. If symptoms worsen, return.
Afternote, for the patient

Your breathing, and what to do before Sunday

Antibiotics
Twice a day for 7 days, with breakfast and evening meal.
Steroids
Once a day for 5 days, with breakfast. Take with food.
Before you travel: this treatment aims to improve your breathing before your trip. If you are not improving by Sunday, contact the surgery before travelling.
