The builds

Four builds. One rule: made for the hardest day.

Each one is small enough to explain in a sentence and built to work when a person has the least energy to spare.

MyScribe Building now with tech partners

Your appointment. Your record. On your phone.

Most of what a clinician says is forgotten straight away. MyScribe records the conversation, summarises it faithfully and keeps it for you, to share with who you choose.

Between 40 and 80 percent of a medical appointment is forgotten immediately (Kessels, 2003).

Afternote In demonstration

Your clinician's words, made readable for your hardest day.

A plain language note from your appointment or your hospital stay, made for reading at home. Every word comes from what your clinician said. Afternote is an open format that any team can adopt.

Neighbourhood Signals Pilot complete

Hear the neighbourhood before it reaches crisis.

Neighbourhood Signals reads neighbourhood activity for themes of unmet need and shows them on a public dashboard, so the Integrated Neighbourhood Team can act early.

CoLab Lens In demonstration

A checking layer for wherever a machine speaks to a person.

Four professional perspectives look through five dimensions of emotional architecture. The Lens works only with what was said and softens clinical language until it helps the person receiving it.

Held by Neighbourhood CoLab CIC, an asset locked community interest company.

  • Analogue to digital
  • Hospital to community

The lens every build passes through

Health, social care and the voluntary sector at one table.

Every build is shaped through an Integrated Neighbourhood Team lens. The people who treat, the people who support and the people who know the community all get to say what good looks like, before anything ships. Every interface explains itself, so a tired person can use it on first touch.

A build that serves people on their hardest day serves them every other day too.

Who is building this
