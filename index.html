Proof of concept: Afternote

Afternote is the Lens at work inside a single conversation — a GP appointment, an assessment, a difficult call. It's your recording, made on your own phone, run through the Lens before anything is kept, and yours to keep afterwards.

01

Ask

You ask, out loud, before anything runs. Your consent to receive it, their consent to be recorded — both, every time.

02

Listen

MyScribe captures the conversation as it happens, in the room, without anyone needing to type.

03

Lens

The CoLab Lens reads it back through four professional voices — accuracy, practical safety, emotional impact, and coherence and meaning.

04

Save

What comes out is your Afternote — what happened, what was agreed, what to watch for, what to do next.

Also proving the Lens: Neighbourhood Signals, listening at the level of a whole community rather than one conversation at a time.
