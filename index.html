VT: Afternote

Afternote sits inside a conversation that already has to happen — a GP appointment, an assessment, a difficult call — and makes sure nothing said in it gets lost afterwards.

01

Ask

Afternote asks permission to record, out loud, at the start — nothing runs without that.

02

Listen

MyScribe captures the conversation as it happens, in the room, without anyone needing to type.

03

Lens

The CoLab Lens reads it back through four professional voices — clinical accuracy, practical safety, emotional impact, and coherence and meaning — before anything is kept.

04

Save

What comes out is your Afternote — what happened, what was agreed, what to watch for, what to do next — yours to revisit anytime.

Caption: built by

Neighbourhood CoLab CIC is four childhood friends from Weymouth and Portland — a nurse, a social worker, a counselling psychologist and an applied philosopher — putting well over a century of frontline experience into deciding what a machine is and isn't allowed to say about a conversation it just heard.

Also in development: Neighbourhood Signals, listening at the level of a whole community rather than one conversation at a time.
