Founder: MyScribe

"Do you mind if I record this?" — the question that opens every MyScribe conversation. Everything about trust follows from getting that right.

↓
The problem

People are already recording the conversations that matter — often covertly. Smart glasses. Wearable recorders. AI chat apps.

Unsupervised. Unreviewed. Ready or not.

Why it matters

40–80%

of what's said in a medical appointment is forgotten immediately.

Kessels, R.P.C. (2003), Journal of the Royal Society of Medicine

1 in 5

GP appointments are for non-medical needs — loneliness, housing, debt, relationships.

Widely cited in NHS social prescribing research

15 million

GP appointments are missed every year in England — costing the NHS over £216 million.

NHS England, General Practice Appointments data

How it works
Record

Start it like a conversation. Ask if it's alright, then let it run in the background.

Summarise

MyScribe turns what was said into a clear, faithful summary — nothing added, nothing implied.

Remember

It's yours to keep, revisit, and share, whenever you need it again.

The line we hold
No interpretation

A record. Not an interpretation.

MyScribe tells you what was said — never what it means, or what to do next.
